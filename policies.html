Community standards

Terms, Policies, and Conditions

These standards protect academic integrity and keep the platform useful.

Students may NOT share instances of their past projects, essays, or long-term academic endeavors relevant to a specific class. If a student attempts to share these materials, they will be flagged by our team and will be removed from the platform. The contributor may also be prevented from sharing materials in the future.

Inappropriate or irrelevant uploads of topics may not be contributed to the platform. Only when a topic is relevant to a math or computer science course or when a specific explanation of a relevant topic is requested by a student may a video or materials on that topic be uploaded.

You DON'T have to have a tutoring profile on our website to upload any resources you think could help another student. If you would like to upload a PDF, video, or helpful resource, even if it is a link on a Google Document, please submit it to this Google Form so that our tutors can ensure that your contribution meets our standards of helping every student succeed.

FOR THE NOVEMBER VIDEO CONTEST

  1. The contest is open to all currently enrolled Montgomery County Public Schools (MCPS) students.
  2. To enter, submit an original, concept-focused educational video (under 5 minutes) in math or computer science via our standard resource submission form.
  3. This is a skill-based competition. Submissions will be evaluated by the MoCoScholar student editorial panel and scored out of 30 total points based on three equal criteria: Technical Accuracy (10 points), Explanational Clarity (10 points), and MCPS Curriculum Alignment (10 points).
  4. In the event of a tie, the submission with the highest individual score in the "Explanational Clarity" category will be declared the winner.
  5. Submissions open immediately and close on November 15th at 11:59 PM EST.
  6. One winner will receive a $50 DoorDash gift card. The prize is non-transferable.
  7. This contest is hosted independently by MoCoScholar. It is not sponsored by, endorsed by, or financially affiliated with Montgomery County Public Schools or DoorDash.
