# MoCoScholar

**By students, for students.**

MoCoScholar is a student-created educational resource platform designed to connect Montgomery County Public Schools (MCPS) students with helpful academic resources created by other students who have already taken their math and computer science courses.

The goal of MoCoScholar is to make it easier for students to learn from one another, share useful explanations, and contribute to a stronger academic community across MCPS.

## About MoCoScholar

MoCoScholar focuses on **math and computer science** courses within the MCPS curriculum.

Students who have taken these courses can contribute:

* Helpful notes and study resources
* Videos explaining concepts
* Specific topic explanations
* Links to useful educational resources
* Other materials that comply with MoCoScholar's academic integrity guidelines

Students looking for help can access these resources through the MoCoScholar database.

## Website Pages

### Home

Introduces MoCoScholar and its goal of creating a student-powered learning community.

### Mission

Explains the mission behind MoCoScholar, introduces its founders, and provides opportunities for students interested in joining the MoCoScholar team.

### Tutors

Introduces participating student tutors, including their school, grade, and subjects or courses in which they are comfortable providing explanations.

Students can also apply to become a MoCoTutor.

### Database

Provides access to the MoCoScholar resource database, where students can find student-created notes, videos, explanations, and other educational materials.

Students can also submit their own resources for review and possible inclusion in the database.

### Terms & Policies

Explains MoCoScholar's academic integrity and content standards.

Students may **not** submit past projects, essays, or other long-term academic work from specific classes for other students to copy or reuse.

Submitted resources are reviewed to help ensure they comply with MoCoScholar's guidelines and those on page 24 of the MCPS Student Code of Conduct (2026-27).

## Contributing Resources

Students do not need to have an official tutor profile to contribute.

Any student with a useful educational resource can submit it for review. Contributions may include PDFs, videos, explanations, Google Documents, or other resources that could help another student understand an academic concept.

Resources are reviewed before being added to the database.

## Hosting

MoCoScholar is hosted using **GitHub Pages**.

Changes made to the repository can be committed to GitHub and deployed to the live website through the project's GitHub Pages configuration.

## Academic Integrity

MoCoScholar is intended to help students **learn concepts**, not provide completed assignments for students to copy.

Contributors should only submit materials that comply with the platform's Terms, Policies, and Conditions. Resources that violate these standards may be removed or rejected.
